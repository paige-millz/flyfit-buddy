# Airframe models

Exterior 3D models shown in the loading scene. All are from the FlightGear add-on
aircraft collection (FGAddon, https://sourceforge.net/p/flightgear/fgaddon/), licensed
GPL v2 or later, converted from AC3D to glTF with textures downscaled to 1024 px.
They are visual references only; the fit engine never reads them.

| File | Aircraft | Author (3D) | FGAddon path |
|---|---|---|---|
| kingair.glb | Beechcraft Super King Air | Emmanuel Baranger | Aircraft/Beechcraft-King-Air |
| pc12.glb | Pilatus PC-12/47 | Emmanuel Baranger | Aircraft/Pilatus-PC-12 |
| caravan.glb | Cessna 208 Caravan (floats removed) | Emmanuel Baranger | Aircraft/Cessna-208-Caravan |
| c310.glb | Cessna 310 (interior objects removed) | FlightGear c310 authors | Aircraft/c310 |
| katana.glb | Diamond DA20 Katana | Emmanuel Baranger | Aircraft/Katana |
| bravo.glb | Cessna 550 Citation Bravo | Syd Adams | Aircraft/Citation-Bravo |

Stand-ins: the King Air model is used for the C90 (same family, shorter), the Citation
Bravo for the CJ1 (550 family, different tail), the Cessna 310 for the 340.
